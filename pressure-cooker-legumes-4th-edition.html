The Dump Pot:
set-and-forget beans & lentils

No soaking rituals, no babysitting a stove, mostly no cutting board. Dry legumes plus a handful of pantry staples go into the pressure cooker, the lid goes on, and you leave. Five ingredients or fewer per recipe, not counting water, salt, or oil. Rounded out with plain and seasoned grains to serve everything over.

HIGH = high pressure · NR = natural release (let it sit before opening) · QR = quick release (vent it immediately)
"No Chop" = everything goes in whole, halved, or smashed · "Light Chop" = a little knife work helps
