With Rice

下饭菜 · 50 Vegetarian Chinese Recipes

Fifty simple, five-ingredient vegetarian dishes built for one purpose: to be eaten spooned over a hot bowl of steamed rice. Mix, match, and rotate — that's the whole cookbook.

How to read "5 ingredients": each recipe lists five main ingredients. Pantry basics — cooking oil, salt, sugar, soy sauce, cornstarch, garlic, and water — are assumed to be on hand and aren't counted toward the five.

Mapo Tofu

Tofu & Bean

Ingredients

  • Soft tofu
  • Doubanjiang (chili bean paste)
  • Fermented black beans
  • Ground Sichuan peppercorn
  • Scallion

Method

  1. Cube the tofu and simmer in hot water for 2 min to firm it up; drain.
  2. Fry a spoonful of doubanjiang and black beans in oil until fragrant and red.
  3. Add a splash of water, slide in the tofu, and simmer 5 min.
  4. Thicken with a cornstarch slurry, top with scallion and ground Sichuan peppercorn.

Scallion Oil Tofu

Tofu & Bean

Ingredients

  • Soft tofu
  • Scallions
  • Light soy sauce
  • Sesame oil
  • Dried chili flakes

Method

  1. Steam a block of soft tofu whole for 8 min; pour off the liquid.
  2. Slice a mountain of scallions and scatter over the tofu.
  3. Heat oil until smoking and pour it over the scallions to sizzle them.
  4. Drizzle with soy sauce and sesame oil, top with chili flakes.

Braised Tofu with Shiitake

Tofu & Bean

Ingredients

  • Firm tofu
  • Dried shiitake mushrooms
  • Soy sauce
  • Star anise
  • Scallion

Method

  1. Rehydrate shiitake in warm water, reserving the soaking liquid.
  2. Pan-fry cubed tofu until lightly golden on all sides.
  3. Add mushrooms, soy sauce, star anise, and the mushroom liquid; simmer 10 min.
  4. Reduce until glossy and finish with scallion.

Home-Style Tofu

Tofu & Bean

Ingredients

  • Fried tofu
  • Napa cabbage
  • Doubanjiang
  • Garlic
  • Scallion

Method

  1. Slice fried tofu and blanch cabbage briefly.
  2. Stir-fry garlic and doubanjiang until fragrant and oil turns red.
  3. Add tofu and cabbage, toss with a splash of water to build a light sauce.
  4. Simmer 3-4 min and finish with scallion.

Tomato Tofu

Tofu & Bean

Ingredients

  • Silken tofu
  • Tomatoes
  • Scallion
  • Sugar
  • Soy sauce

Method

  1. Cut tomatoes into wedges and cook down in oil until saucy.
  2. Season with soy sauce and a pinch of sugar to balance acidity.
  3. Gently slide in cubed silken tofu, simmer 4 min without stirring hard.
  4. Top with scallion before serving.

Crispy Tofu with Chili Salt

Tofu & Bean

Ingredients

  • Firm tofu
  • Cornstarch
  • Five-spice powder
  • Salt
  • Scallion

Method

  1. Cube tofu, pat dry, and toss lightly in cornstarch.
  2. Shallow-fry until golden and crisp on all sides.
  3. Toss hot tofu with a mix of salt and five-spice powder.
  4. Scatter chopped scallion over the top and serve immediately.

Tofu with Black Bean Sauce

Tofu & Bean

Ingredients

  • Firm tofu
  • Fermented black beans
  • Garlic
  • Bell pepper
  • Scallion

Method

  1. Pan-fry tofu cubes until golden; set aside.
  2. Mash fermented black beans with garlic and fry until fragrant.
  3. Add bell pepper strips and tofu, toss to coat in the sauce.
  4. Finish with scallion and a splash of water to loosen the sauce.

Garlic Bok Choy

Leafy Greens

Ingredients

  • Baby bok choy
  • Garlic
  • Vegetarian oyster-flavor sauce
  • Sesame oil
  • Salt

Method

  1. Halve or quarter baby bok choy lengthwise; rinse well.
  2. Blanch briefly in salted boiling water until just bright green.
  3. Fry sliced garlic in oil until golden and fragrant.
  4. Pour garlic oil and vegetarian oyster sauce over the bok choy.
